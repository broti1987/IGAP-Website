Our foundation
IGAP was founded to bring rigorous research, strategic advisory, and meaningful dialogue into one policy platform. Built at the intersection of technology, governance, and society, it was created to make policy work more applied, interdisciplinary, and relevant to real-world decision-making.
Our mission
Our mission is to advance inclusive, evidence-based policymaking by connecting research, advisory, and convening. We work to help shape governance that is practical, future-facing, and grounded in people’s needs
